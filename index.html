What GapCheck is and isn't

  • What it does: statistical keyword extraction (RAKE-style) from the posting, then tolerant matching against your resume — stemming ("counts" = "counting"), word-order tolerance ("management of projects" = "project management"), and acronym equivalence parsed from the posting itself ("WMS" = "Warehouse Management System").
  • What it doesn't do: there is no AI model here and no server. It can't judge your bullet points, predict any employer's screening software, or see the actual ATS. The score is a heuristic estimate of keyword coverage — a gap-finder, not a prediction.
  • Privacy: everything runs in this page. Your texts are saved only in this browser's local storage (so you don't lose them on refresh) and never sent anywhere. Use Clear to wipe them.
  • Limits: English text only; pasted plain text (it can't read PDF/DOCX files itself — copy the text out first); acronyms are only understood when the posting defines them or they're in a tiny built-in list.
